# Module 2: Medication Research

## Purpose
Research all prescribed medications and supplements — evidence base, risks, alternatives, availability, and prices.

## For Each Medication/Supplement

### Research Checklist
1. **What is it?** Active ingredient, mechanism of action, class
2. **Evidence base** — key RCTs, meta-analyses supporting its use for THIS patient's condition
3. **Dose assessment** — is the prescribed dose appropriate per clinical guidelines?
4. **Risks and side effects** — especially relevant to THIS patient's profile (kidney, testosterone, etc.)
5. **Drug interactions** — with other medications and supplements the patient takes
6. **Regulatory status** — medication (GRLS) vs supplement (BAD) in patient's country
7. **Availability** — pharmacy chains, online pharmacies
8. **Price** — monthly cost, with links to purchase
9. **Alternatives** — if the medication is questionable, what are evidence-based alternatives?

### Output Structure
- Individual research file per drug class: `/research/medication_[name].md`
- Consolidated analysis: `/analysis/prescription_analysis.md`
- Price comparison table
- Drug interaction matrix

### Special Considerations
- If patient refuses a drug class (e.g., statins) — research alternatives with same level of detail
- Compare BAD vs registered medication options
- Note if a medication is not available in patient's country
- For supplements: check if claims are supported by RCTs, not just marketing
